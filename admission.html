Admission Process & Guidelines

Admissions at MBD College Doora are conducted annually based on academic merit and criteria approved by the administrative board. Candidates must submit their application files on time to secure their seats.

Eligibility Criteria (Placeholder)

[This subsection remains open for administrative edits. Exact qualifying marks, age limits, and subject requirements for streams will be defined once finalized by Dr. S.P. Verma and the college board.]

* Candidates generally require a pass certificate from a recognized board (10+2 secondary or equivalent) to qualify for applying.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Original and copy of 10th Standard Marks Card (DOB proof).
  • Original and copy of 12th Standard (10+2) Marks Card.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school/college.
  • Character Certificate signed by the head of the previous institute.
  • Recent passport-sized color photographs (4 copies).
  • Category / Caste Certificate (if seeking reservation benefit).
  • Domicile certificate (if applicable).

Step-by-Step Admission Process

1
Form Submission: Fill out the application form (available at the college office or through this online enquiry portal).
2
Document Verification: Present original marks sheets and certificates at the college administration office for verification.
3
Merit List Announcement: A qualified list of students based on eligibility guidelines will be displayed on the notice board.
4
Fee Payment: Pay the designated admission fee within the given time frames to lock and secure your seat.
